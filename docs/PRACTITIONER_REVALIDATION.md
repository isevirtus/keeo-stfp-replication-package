# Practitioner revalidation of V2

This release records the aggregate outcomes reported for 40 blinded comparison
cards reviewed by the same staffing authority who participated in knowledge
elicitation. The exercise compared refined V2 GA recommendations with 16
staffing-LLM teams, 12 historical teams, and 12 teams generated by a GA using
the uniform objective. Provenance and evaluator scores were hidden during the
review.

Four historical comparisons were omitted because the historical rosters
coincided with the retained LLM rosters. The uniform-objective condition used
the retained 12-case subset. For each GA condition, the displayed team was the
best valid result among 30 runs under that condition's own objective, with the
lowest seed breaking exact ties. Uniform-objective teams were rescored only
after selection.

The response instrument distinguished clear preference, slight preference
while both teams remained acceptable, and no meaningful preference while both
teams remained acceptable. It also allowed optional reasons. The public package
does not contain the card-level responses or comments.

## Reported aggregate outcomes

- LLM condition: both teams were acceptable in 14 of 16 comparisons. Eleven
  involved a slight preference and three involved no meaningful preference.
  The released aggregate does not identify the direction of the 11 slight
  preferences or the disposition of the remaining two comparisons.
- Historical-team condition: seven historical-team preferences, three refined
  V2 GA preferences, and two equivalences across 12 comparisons.
- Uniform-GA condition: four refined V2 GA preferences, two uniform-GA
  preferences, and six equivalences across 12 comparisons. One equivalence
  involved identical team composition and functions as a control.

The machine-readable counterpart is
`data/v2/practitioner_revalidation_summary.csv`.

## Interpretation boundaries

These counts come from one authority, reuse project contexts and comparator
rosters, and use a response scale that differs from the earlier review. They do
not constitute independent observations, a controlled before/after treatment
effect, or statistical equivalence. Without the row-level cards, the public
package cannot reproduce presentation order, A/B position, project-level
judgments, optional comments, or response timing.
