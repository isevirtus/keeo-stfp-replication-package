# Expert follow-up and policy-validation boundaries

This account summarizes further elicitation from the same participant who
contributed to the STFP knowledge model. It is not an independent-expert sample,
an optimization experiment, or a third implemented knowledge version.

## Participant and decision context

The participant reported at least 25 years of experience in team formation or
personnel allocation. The participant took part in final team-composition
decisions, consulted project managers about specific needs and competing
requests for personnel, and described final decisions as shared with managers.
Routine managerial consultation does not make this a multi-expert elicitation
study or establish organizational consensus.

An October 2026 Portuguese-language follow-up questionnaire contained 14 items combining
closed alternatives and open responses. It covered experience and decision
roles, technology preparation, collaboration saturation, missing history,
possible adjustments, and permission for non-nominal characterization.
One response was obtained. Existing parameters and explanations were named
in the questions, so the responses are retrospective rationale and refinement
input, not independent parameter estimates.

## Collaboration policy

| Element | Elicited rationale or observation | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Missing history | Absence of co-participation is not incompatibility; a cautious Low default avoids automatic exclusion of new pairs. | Supports the intended meaning of the default, not observed compatibility. |
| Repeated collaboration | Initial engagement and knowledge exchange can give way to comfort or complacency and additional managerial effort; reduced critical appraisal was one consideration. | A local policy rationale, not an established universal causal relationship. |
| Saturation peak | The question named four projects; the open answer described a peak around the second and third projects of the same team. | The precise peak and operationalization through pairwise counts require clarification. |
| New versus saturated pairs | The comparison was considered context-dependent; a floor above 0.3 for pairs with history and explicit separation of missing history from saturation were suggested. | No exact replacement floor or function was specified; the proposal is not incorporated into V2. |

The original no-history value is the ordinal Low value 0.3, not the neutral
point 0.5. Under the V2 compression, it becomes 0.4. Raw saturation values,
ordinal centroids, and the compressed scale are distinct. The questionnaire's
0.3-versus-0.25 scenario must not be treated as a verified comparison of final
V2 team scores.

Any adopted change must be identified separately from the V2 specification,
with its rationale, numerical rule, challenge cases, affected implementations,
and renewed computational evaluation. Existing V1 and V2 records remain
historical evidence for their original versions.

## Technical preparation and profile snapshots

Organizational practice included preparation for technologies of a specific
project, consideration of related technology experience, and close mentoring
by a more technically and managerially experienced professional during
velocity-calibration sprints. Competence validation, practical experience,
and project context could inform later candidate eligibility.

These practices are context, not implemented training optimization. The
evaluated STFP uses recorded profiles and team-level Must coverage. A person's
unrecorded experience or ability to acquire a skill must not be silently
treated as already present in the experimental snapshot.

## Consent and release scope

The participant agreed to non-nominal characterization of experience and role
in the thesis and derived publications. That agreement does not by itself
establish permission to process or publish personnel records, original reports,
or identifiable interaction logs. This release contains a narrative summary,
not the original response sheet or consent record.

The historical data-preparation and end-to-end reproduction boundaries are
documented separately in [REPRODUCIBILITY.md](REPRODUCIBILITY.md). All released
experimental data and analysis scripts are unchanged by this documentation
update.
